## QuickBite Express: Providing Insights for Crisis Recovery in an Online Food Delivery Startup 

## Project Overview
This project analyzes the post-crisis recovery of **QuickBite Express**, a Bengaluru-based food-tech startup. Following a major operational and reputational crisis in **June 2025** (food safety incidents and delivery outages), this analysis leverages data to:
*   **Track recovery efficiency**
*   **Regain customer trust**
*   **Optimize a major recovery budget**

---

## The Crisis Impact
*   **User Disengagement:** Large portion of active users left the platform[cite: 2].
*   **Order Decline:** Daily orders saw a sharp drop compared to previous months[cite: 2].
*   **Trust Issues:** Customer satisfaction scores fell sharply[cite: 2].
