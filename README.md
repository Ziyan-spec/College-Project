## Providing Insights for Crisis Recovery in an Online Food Delivery Startup 

## Project Overview
This project analyzes the post-crisis recovery of **QuickBite Express**, a Bengaluru-based food-tech startup. Following a major operational and reputational crisis in **June 2025** (food safety incidents and delivery outages), this analysis leverages data to:
*   **Track recovery efficiency**
*   **Regain customer trust**
*   **Optimize a major recovery budget**

---

## The Crisis Impact
*   **User Disengagement:** Large portion of active users left the platform.
*   **Order Decline:** Daily orders saw a sharp drop compared to previous months.
*   **Trust Issues:** Customer satisfaction scores fell sharply.

## Tech Stack & Methodology
*   **Analysis:** Python (Pandas, NumPy)
*   **Database:** Star Schema (Fact & Dimension Tables)
*   **Visualization:** Matplotlib & Seaborn
